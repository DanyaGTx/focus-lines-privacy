Focus Lines Privacy Policy

App covered — Focus Lines for Android (com.focuslines.game).
Publisher — Popolino Studio
Privacy inquiries — danylo.popolino@gmail.com
Effective date — 18 November 2026

Information stored on the device

Focus Lines stores completed levels, unlocked levels, and preferences such as language, theme, sound, music volume and motion settings in private app storage. The game reads these files to resume your progress and apply your settings. This information stays in the app's private storage. The game has no account sign-up or developer-operated cloud sync. Android backup is disabled in the app manifest.

Google Play purchases

Focus Lines uses Google Play Billing for its optional, one-time Pro purchase. The game asks Google Play for product and price information and checks Pro ownership, including when the game starts, resumes, or you choose Restore Purchases. Google Play processes payments and can process information about the Google account, device, purchase and payment method under Google's own terms. The game does not receive your card number, bank details, Google password or full payment account details. Google Privacy Policy

Google Play returns purchase and entitlement information, including a signed purchase record that can contain product and order details, purchase time, status and a purchase token. The app uses that information to verify ownership and, where needed, sends the purchase token back to Google Play to acknowledge the purchase. The app keeps the signed receipt in its private storage so it can confirm Pro access offline. Receipt verification checks authenticity. The game sends neither progress nor receipts to a developer-operated server. Google retains and processes its own transaction records separately. Google Play Billing

Permissions and third-party services

  • Internet and network-state access support billing and connection handling. The puzzle boards and their assets are installed with the game.
  • The Google Play Billing permission lets the app request purchase and restoration services.
  • Android uses an app-specific, signature-level permission to protect internal broadcast handling.
  • This Android build contains no ad or analytics SDK. Its manifest requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts or shared-file access.

Retention and deletion

The app retains your progress and preferences for as long as the local app data remains. Settings → Reset progress clears campaign progress. To remove settings and the local purchase receipt as well, clear Focus Lines storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. An online ownership check can also remove the cached receipt if Google Play reports that Pro is no longer owned.

Removing local data does not cancel a purchase or remove Google's records. You can use Google Play to manage the purchase, and a valid purchase can be restored using the same Google Play account. For privacy inquiries, use the developer contact listed at the top of this policy.

## Children and families

Focus Lines is intended for a broad audience, including children. The game has no chat, user profiles, advertisements, analytics SDK, or developer-operated account service. Progress and preferences stay on the device. The optional Pro purchase is processed by Google Play under its own terms and parental purchase-approval settings. A parent or guardian can contact us using the email above with a privacy question.

Security and policy updates

Purchase requests use the Google Play Billing service and its protected transport. The game verifies signed purchase records before granting Pro access and stores the receipt in private app storage. We update this page when our data practices change and identify the effective date above.
